Text Message Program & Consent

Effective date: September 2026

This page documents exactly how Winners Mark collects consent to send text messages, and what those messages say. It is published so that customers, and the carriers who review messaging programs, can see the full opt-in flow.

Program description

Winners Mark, a dba of High Tech Bowling Pro Shop, LLC, operates a bowling pro shop inside Valley Lanes at 3951 W 5400 S, West Valley City, UT 84120. We send two kinds of transactional text message, and no others:

  • Purchase receipts. A one-time message confirming a purchase made in person at our counter, listing what was bought and the total.
  • Special order arrival notices. A message telling a customer that an item they ordered and paid for has arrived and is ready for pickup or fitting.

We do not send marketing, promotional, or recurring campaign messages of any kind.

How consent is collected

There is no website form. Every opt-in happens in person, at our counter, at the moment of a sale. A staff member asks the customer whether they want a text receipt, reads the disclosure below aloud, and only then types the customer's phone number into our point-of-sale system. A customer who does not want a text simply says no, and no number is entered.

Verbal script used by staff

"Would you like your receipt by text message? If so, I'll need your mobile number.

By giving me your number, you're agreeing to receive a one-time automated text receipt from Winners Mark for this purchase, and a message if you have a special order arriving. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

This is completely optional — it's not required to buy anything, and I can print you a paper receipt instead."

Written disclosure shown on the point-of-sale screen

The same disclosure appears on screen, directly beneath the phone number field, visible to the customer as the number is entered:

"By providing a phone number, the customer agrees to receive a text message receipt from Winners Mark. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."

The phone number field is blank by default. Nothing is pre-filled, pre-checked, or entered on the customer's behalf. A number is typed only after the customer has said yes.

Message frequency, cost, and opting out

  • Frequency: varies. Messages are sent only in response to a purchase or a special order arriving — typically one per transaction.
  • Cost: Winners Mark does not charge for these messages. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for assistance. You may also ask a staff member in person to remove your number from our system.
  • Consent is never required as a condition of any purchase. A printed receipt is always available instead.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party. Full details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

Winners Mark, a dba of High Tech Bowling Pro Shop, LLC ("Winners Mark," "we," "us," or "our"), operates a bowling pro shop located inside Valley Lanes at 3951 W 5400 S, Taylorsville, UT 84120. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

Information We Collect

When you make a purchase or interact with our shop, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number (if you choose to provide it, for example to receive a text receipt)
  • Details of the products or services you purchase

We do not require a phone number to make a purchase. Providing one is always optional and is only used for the purposes described below.

How We Use Your Information

  • To complete and record your purchase
  • To send you a text message receipt, if you provide a phone number and request one
  • To notify you about the status of a special order, if applicable
  • To respond to questions you ask us directly

Text Messaging (SMS)

If you provide your phone number, you may receive transactional text messages from Winners Mark, such as purchase receipts or order status updates. Message frequency varies based on your purchases. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive text messages is never required as a condition of making a purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How We Do Not Use Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain purchase and contact records as needed to operate our business, respond to inquiries, and comply with applicable recordkeeping and tax obligations.

Your Choices

You may ask us at any time to stop contacting you, to tell you what information we have on file for you, or to remove your contact information from our records, by reaching out using the contact information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
